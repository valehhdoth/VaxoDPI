Why VaxoDPI

No remote server. Traffic is never sent to a VPN server, and your IP address does not change.

Fast. The bypass is applied only to servers that appear to be blocked, so other sites are not slowed down.

Private. No analytics, no ads, no accounts. An optional local log is off by default.

Your choice of apps and DNS. Send only selected apps through the tunnel and pick Cloudflare, Google, Quad9 or AdGuard DNS.

How it works

Android's VpnService passes device traffic to a local tunnel, which forwards it to VaxoProxy, our own SOCKS5 engine running on your phone. For HTTPS it splits the TLS handshake and TCP segments so that DPI systems cannot recognize the connection.

Install

  1. Download the APK from the latest release.
  2. Open it and allow installation.
  3. Android may show a Play Protect warning because the app is not distributed through the Play Store. Tap "More details", then "Install anyway".
